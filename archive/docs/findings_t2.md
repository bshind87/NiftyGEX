Generated by `scripts/write_findings_t2.py` from the results store (`outputs/results/`); every number below comes from a stored run. Data config hash `9fc36c738aa5`, eval config hash `51e34cd2d489`.

**Target.** T2 compares next-day realized volatility with its trailing 20-day level, measured from front-month futures 5-minute returns (intraday only) at the 15:15 snapshot. Labels are causal terciles and start in 2020. See `docs/schema.md`.

**Finding: the inferred sign adds information beyond magnitude; the naive sign does not.** When the Pro dealer proxy is net long gamma (positive inferred total GEX), the next-day RV ratio is lower, the direction the dampening hypothesis predicts. The effect holds before the weekly-expiry rationalisation marker but is not significant after it, on a much smaller post sample. The inferred sign comes from daily participant OI covering all index options, so it may proxy participant positioning rather than NIFTY gamma itself.

**Findings**

- **VIX alone has no skill**, because the target is relative to its own trailing level.
- **Untuned models are overconfident.** Their Brier score is no better than the class-frequency prior.
- **Regularization fixes most of the calibration.** It is chosen on a time-ordered inner validation split.
- **Platt scaling then makes the reference model beat the prior** on Brier in most walks.

**Findings**

- **Participant positioning adds nothing** over vol state and calendar (B vs A).
- **Levels, not GEX magnitude, carry the C gain.** On IC, C2 (levels + concentration) improves on B by 0.0333 (wins 19/26, p 0.019), as much as the full set C does (0.0340, p 0.017). C1 (normalized GEX + inferred sign) adds 0.0028 (p 0.794).
- **After Holm within F2, no comparison is significant at 5%.** C vs B has Holm p 0.067 and C2 vs B has 0.067. These are suggestive, not confirmed.
- **F1 inferred-sign result:** full-sample coefficient -0.031, Holm p 0.012 (survives at 5%). Sign terms rejecting under Holm: inferred sign, full, inferred sign, pre_weekly_expiry_rationalisation.
- **SHAP agrees with the ablation.** Vol-state features dominate. Among GEX/levels features, max-pain distance and GEX concentration rank highest. SHAP measures use, not incremental value, so participant features can rank high in SHAP while adding nothing in the ablation.
- **Most of the predictability comes from vol-state features**, chiefly volatility mean reversion.
- **Caveat:** with about 20 walks and several comparisons, t-statistics around 2 are modest evidence.
