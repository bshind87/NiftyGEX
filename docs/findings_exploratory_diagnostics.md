## Summary

- 9 of 9 registered E1 tests reject at 5% after Holm.
- The chain adds information beyond HAR: models that see no volatility state predict HAR's out-of-sample residual with a mean per-walk IC of 0.204 to 0.218 (positive in 24-25 of 26 walks).
- Adding that correction to HAR raises IC by +0.030 to +0.046.
- The hybrid (HAR forecast + learned attention + GRU correction) reaches IC 0.492, +0.047 over attention + GRU on the same V100s (95% CI +0.008 to +0.085).
- The equal-weight combination of HAR, LightGBM and attention + GRU is the best E1 model: IC 0.502, +0.068 over HAR.
- Inside the neural trainer the tokens add little: the state-only MLP (no tokens) reaches IC 0.459 against 0.445 for attention + GRU (state-only +0.015, 95% CI -0.034 to +0.063, not significant), and QLIKE 0.200 against 0.188 (HAR 0.223): most of the neural QLIKE gain comes from the trainer and the state, not the chain.
- No GEX regime, flip proximity or dealer-sign subset changes the picture beyond its 95% interval; expiry days are harder for every model (combination 0.466 vs 0.554 on other days).
