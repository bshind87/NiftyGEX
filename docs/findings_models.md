## 1. The ladder (steps 0–6)

How to read the scores:

- **RMSE**: typical size of a forecast's miss, in units of the target (log of tomorrow's volatility over its recent normal); lower is better.
- **QLIKE**: the standard error measure for volatility forecasts: it compares forecast and realized variance as a ratio and punishes under-predicting a volatile day more than over-predicting it by the same amount; lower is better.
- **IC**: ranking accuracy: whether the forecasts put calm and wild days in the right order (rank correlation, 1 = perfect, 0 = none); higher is better.

**Why QLIKE?**

- What it is: QLIKE compares the variance that happened (s) with the variance forecast (h): QLIKE = s/h − log(s/h) − 1. It is 0 for a perfect forecast and grows with the miss; we report its average over test days, lower is better.
- It punishes under-prediction more: forecasting tomorrow's volatility 20% too low costs 0.116, 20% too high costs 0.059, so under-predicting costs about 2.0 times as much. Being caught out by a wild day is the costlier mistake for anyone managing risk.
- It measures misses as ratios, so a 20% miss counts the same on a calm day and on a wild day; RMSE on our log target does the same.
- It is the standard loss for comparing volatility forecasts: it is one of the few losses shown (Patton, 2011) to rank forecasts correctly even when the true volatility can only be measured with noise, as here by realized volatility from 5-minute returns.

## 2. RQ1: does reading every contract predict better?

**Attention + GRU performs as well as the best classical models: it ranks next-day volatility on par with HAR-RV, HAR + GEX and tuned LightGBM (IC 0.447) and clearly better than persistence.** On forecast error it has the lowest RMSE of all 8 models (0.285), significantly lower than persistence by 23% (p < 0.001) and tuned LightGBM by 5% (p 0.015); and the lowest QLIKE of all 8 models (0.189), significantly lower than persistence by 50% (p < 0.001), HAR + GEX by 19% (p 0.033) and tuned LightGBM by 16% (p 0.042).

Against GEX (HAR + GEX) it ties on ranking (+0.001 IC, 95% interval -0.050 to +0.053) and beats it on forecast error: QLIKE 19% lower (p 0.033); RMSE 1.7% lower, not significant (p 0.29).

The pre-registered win rule (a significant IC gain over every classical model) is not met: the IC gains over HAR-RV, HAR + GEX and tuned LightGBM are smaller than the test can detect (gains above +0.060 are ruled out). So the model does well, but not by the clear margin we hoped for.
