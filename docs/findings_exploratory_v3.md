- A1. HAR-IV (HAR plus log ATM IV) vs HAR: +0.034 IC (95% CI +0.015 to +0.054, Holm p 0.0079, rejects; 20/26 walks). 4 of 1556 test rows have no ATM IV and use plain HAR.
- A1. HAR-IV + chain correction vs HAR-IV: +0.047 IC (95% CI +0.022 to +0.071, Holm p 0.005, rejects; 21/26 walks). **The chain adds information beyond implied volatility: the chain correction still improves on HAR-IV.**
- A2. Token corrections without the IV field, vs HAR: DeepSets +0.025 IC (95% CI -0.002 to +0.052, Holm p 0.068, does not reject; 18/26 walks); attention +0.039 IC (95% CI +0.014 to +0.064, Holm p 0.012, rejects; 21/26 walks). Change from dropping IV (vs the E1 versions with IV, unadjusted): DeepSets -0.005 (95% CI -0.018 to +0.009), attention -0.003 (95% CI -0.015 to +0.009). Gamma, delta and strike GEX still depend on IV, so IV's influence is not fully removed; A1 is the separating test.
- A3. Combination vs tuned LightGBM: +0.055 IC (95% CI +0.020 to +0.089, Holm p 0.012, rejects; 20/26 walks); vs HAR-IV: +0.034 IC (95% CI +0.014 to +0.053, Holm p 0.0079, rejects; 22/26 walks).
- A4. E3 attention + GRU with patience 8 and at most 60 epochs vs the best classical rung: 1 h (Tuned LightGBM) -0.046 IC (95% CI -0.070 to -0.023, Holm p 0.0036, rejects; 5/25 walks); 2 h (intraday HAR) -0.049 IC (95% CI -0.085 to -0.012, Holm p 0.022, rejects; 9/25 walks). Against the patience-3 runs (unadjusted): 1 h +0.000 (95% CI -0.009 to +0.009), 2 h -0.006 (95% CI -0.015 to +0.003); mean stopping epoch 1h: patience 3 2.8, patience 8 4.2, 2h: patience 3 2.7, patience 8 3.6.
