## Design note

As registered, the neural rungs' state vector is the T2 volatility state recomputed at each snapshot plus time of day; it does not include the backward-window RVs (last 30 and 60 minutes, yesterday's same window, the clock-window normal) that the intraday HAR uses. Part of the gap to the classical rungs may therefore come from inputs rather than architecture; a hybrid with the intraday HAR forecast as an input (as in E1) would separate the two and is not registered.
